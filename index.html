Political Science Student 📚

I am a political science student at the University of California, Riverside, set to earn my bachelor's degree soon. I am drawn to the study of politics because laws, institutions, and public decisions shape almost every part of daily life, and I want to understand how they work well enough to help improve them. My coursework has taught me to read closely, argue clearly, and look at a problem from several sides before taking a position, and those habits are the foundation of the legal career I am working toward.

My goal is to land a law internship where I can see the profession up close. I want to learn how attorneys research issues, prepare cases, and communicate with clients, and I am ready to contribute through careful research, strong writing, and a steady work ethic. I am especially interested in how the legal system serves ordinary people and communities, and I hope an internship will help me decide which area of law fits me best.

Outside the classroom, I have built experience in jobs that taught me skills no lecture can. As a waiter at Nick the Greek, I work in a fast-paced environment where I listen to customers, handle pressure calmly, and stay organized while serving many people at once. That job taught me how to communicate under pressure, solve problems quickly, and treat every person with respect, which are qualities that matter just as much in a law office as in a restaurant.

I am also a staff member at Prolific Kids Club LLC, where I work with children and help create a positive, safe, and engaging environment. Working with kids has made me more patient, responsible, and creative, and it has shown me the value of being a dependable role model.

On campus, I am a member of the UCR Coptic Club, a community that gives me a place to connect with others who share my faith and culture. Being part of the club has strengthened my teamwork and my commitment to service, and it keeps me grounded while I balance school and work.

Taken together, my studies, my jobs, and my community involvement reflect what I care about most: hard work, service to others, and a lasting commitment to fairness. Thank you for visiting my site. Why Law

Studying political science has shown me how much of our daily lives is shaped by laws and by the people who interpret and apply them. Courts, legislatures, and agencies decide questions about rights, safety, fairness, and opportunity, and I want to be someone who understands those systems well enough to help people move through them. Law appeals to me because it combines research, writing, and speaking with real responsibility to the people being served.

My work experience has reinforced this interest. Serving customers and working with families has shown me that people often come to professionals during stressful moments and need someone who listens carefully, explains things clearly, and treats them with respect. I believe those same qualities make a good attorney. I also know that the legal profession demands discipline, accuracy, and integrity, and I try to practice all three in my studies and my jobs.

A law internship would give me the chance to learn how legal work actually happens, from researching statutes and case law to drafting documents and supporting a legal team. I am eager to learn from experienced attorneys, take on any task given to me with care, and use the experience to shape my path toward law school and a career in the field.
